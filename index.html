How it works
Three steps, five seconds

Press the hotkey, then select an area

Hit Ctrl+Shift+B (or your custom shortcut) from anywhere, then drag to select the part of your screen you want to share. It opens in the editor with everything blurred, so sensitive data is hidden before you show a single pixel.

Paint to reveal the parts you need

Hold the left mouse button and brush over what you want to show. Adjust brush size and shape. Use Ctrl+Z to undo any brushstroke.

Press Enter — done

The result is saved to a folder and copied to your clipboard automatically. Paste it anywhere, instantly.

FAQ
Common questions
How do I hide sensitive information before sharing a screenshot?
Take the screenshot with BlurSnap and the whole image is blurred instantly — passwords, emails, account numbers and any personal data are hidden by default. Then paint over only the parts you actually want to show and save. Nothing sensitive is revealed unless you choose to reveal it.
Can I blur or redact just part of a screenshot?
Yes — that's exactly what BlurSnap is built for. Instead of hunting down every detail to blur (and risking you miss one), it blurs everything and lets you reveal only what you need with a brush. The sensitive areas stay redacted by default.
Is there a subscription?
No. BlurSnap is a one-time purchase. You get lifetime access and all future updates for free.
Which Windows versions are supported?
Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).
Does it need an internet connection?
Only to activate your license key and for occasional license validation. Your screenshots are never uploaded anywhere.
Can I use it on a new computer?
Yes. Open Settings, click "Deactivate", then install BlurSnap on your new machine and enter the same key. One active device at a time.
What if I lose my license key?
Your key is sent to your email right after purchase. Check your inbox or spam folder. Still can't find it? Contact us at support@blursnap.app.
Why does Windows show a security warning?
As a new indie app, BlurSnap doesn't yet have a Microsoft code-signing certificate. Click "More info" → "Run anyway" to proceed. No telemetry, no ads, no surprises. You can also view the VirusTotal report or scan it yourself for peace of mind.
